This project investigates how Large Language Models (LLMs) can be leveraged to enable task execution in IoT systems, where natural language instructions are interpreted, translated into executable actions, and mapped to device operations. To explore this, a small-scale IoT system will be developed in which multiple heterogeneous IoT devices are coordinated via an LLM interface.

Research

I am interested in causal reasoning for large language models (LLMs): how models can move beyond pattern recognition to form causal understanding and support decision-making for real-world tasks.

LLM Causal Reasoning

Inspired by Judea Pearl's Causality: Models, Reasoning, and Inference, I hope to contribute to advancing causal reasoning in LLMs.
